The archival draft's solution-preserving and downstream-utility framing was replaced. The unedited September 10 registration's statement that geometric positive clearance “certifies” physical clearance is explicitly rejected here: obstacle-present labels must come from closed-loop execution. Historical replay is a measured null; complete-continuation teaching was not isolated by the five-arm study. Neither source papers nor planner reconstruction objectives supply missing physical evidence.

Primary literature checked for the revision: [LfH](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.14479), [LfLH](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.09793), [Dyna-LfLH](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17231), [SONIC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07820). These support related-work descriptions, not this pipeline's outcomes.
