# Goal/map navigation on the local stack: 24 stopping tasks, motor demonstrations and a switch-fraction recovery sweep

September 13–14, 2026, shared RTX 5090 host. This study rebuilds the [navigation pilot](NAVIGATION_MOTOR_PILOT_20260913.md) and [supported-recovery](NAVIGATION_SUPPORTED_RECOVERY_20260913.md) protocol on the locally trained stack: the 8192-environment teacher and the [8192-distilled motor student](DISTILL_8192_TEACHER_20260913.md) (`s1-3200`). It widens the task panel from 8 to 24 stopping tasks, then measures how recovery yield depends on when the frozen motor takes over from the navigation learner.

The headline is unchanged from the source study: exact-motor demonstrations and supported recoveries move the goal/map adapter from 0/24 to 4/24 on one seed, which is the same order as their 0/8 → 4/8, and the confirmation seed drops it to 1/24 exactly as theirs dropped to 1/8. The new, quantitative finding is the **switch-fraction curve**: takeovers at 15% of the demonstration are supported 18/19 times, at 75% only 3/19. The learner leaves the region the nominal-clock motor can correct within the first third of each task.

## Panel

24 tasks: 12 repaired training motions × {clear, corridor}. The four motions from the source study (00908, 00413, 00976, 00265), its five expanded motions (00399, 00770, 00677, 00120, 00707) and three long low-tail-speed motions (00801, 00781, 00711). Each task appends a 2 s stationary tail, uses the unchanged scorer (3D pelvis distance ≤ 0.25 m and speed ≤ 0.10 m/s for 50 consecutive ticks, prohibited contact > 1 N or pelvis below 0.25 m terminates) and the original deadline. Teacher and motor agree on every task outcome.

Fork gains and losses at seed 91260, recovery versus replay: gains 00677-clear, 00707-clear, 00707-corridor, 00976-corridor; loses 00265-clear and 00908-corridor. Two more recovery-fork attempts end inside the goal with 39- and 33-tick holds (00908 clear/corridor). Both forks share nav-v1's initialization, optimizer reset, seed 91470, loss and frozen motor; only the sampling mixture differs, and as in the source study the mixture also reweights motions, so this is a teaching-package comparison rather than an isolated data-quality effect.

**Seed-91262 confirmation:** the recovery fork completes **1/24** (00908-clear, which it had missed at 91260 by 11 ticks); all four seed-91260 successes fail, three attempts reach the goal, three end in corridor contact, none falls. This mirrors the source study's 4/8 → 1/8 confirmation. The seed-91260 gain is therefore not established; evaluation-seed sensitivity is as large as the effect. The replay fork's seed-91262 panel was still running when this report was committed and is not included.

## Recovery sweep

nav-v1 drove every teacher-passed task to a switch tick, then the frozen motor continued on the nominal reference clock; a suffix is admitted only if it earns its own 50-tick hold before the original deadline without contact. Switch ticks are fractions of the teacher's own completion tick.

| Takeover at | Supported | Rows |
|---|---:|---:|
| 15% | 18/19 | 5,574 |
| 30% | 15/19 | 3,726 |
| 50% | 7/19 | 979 |
| 75% | 3/19 | 205 |
| Total | 43/76 | 10,484 |

Combined with the 19 motor demonstrations, the recovery view holds 62 episodes and 17,653 rows over 10 motions; the source study's comparable view had 17 episodes and 4,620 rows over 4 motions. Yield falls monotonically with the switch fraction because the nominal clock keeps advancing while the learner falls behind or overshoots; by 50% most suffixes can no longer reach and hold the goal before the deadline. Late switches also produce the least useful rows (short tails, mostly standing).

## Why the adapter still fails

Trace analysis of the nav-v1 panel separates three modes:

- **Overshoot without braking** (00908 ×2, 00677, 00707-corridor, 00976-clear): the learner enters the goal tolerance at 0.5–1.0 m/s where the teacher enters below 0.1 m/s, passes through, and ends 0.4–1.2 m beyond it. This is the dominant failure on short tasks.
- **Under-reach on long motions** (00120, 00801, 00781): the learner stops 2–7 m short; the command it emits no longer resembles any phase of the demonstration.
- **Lateral drift into corridor walls** (00677, 00707, 00711 corridor; contact): the map input does not keep the learner centred.

Recoveries fix the first mode on some tasks (the four gains are all short tasks) and not the second: no long-motion task completes in any navigation arm. The 15%-switch rows cover approach and braking from states close to the demonstration; nothing in the data covers re-entry after a large drift, which the source study also identifies as the missing behaviour.

## What this adds to the framework

- **Recovery yield is a measurable function of switch timing.** Registering the switch as a fraction of the reference arm's completion tick makes the curve comparable across tasks and studies; it is the natural knob for a short-cycle navigation DAgger loop (switch early and often rather than at 50%).
- **Larger panel, same conclusion.** 24 tasks and 10 motions reproduce the source study's ordering (teacher rows < motor demos < recoveries) and its ceiling; more of the same data does not close the gap to the motor's 19/24.
- **The pipeline is portable.** `scripts/navigation_distill` stages tasks, collections, fits and panels from any teacher/motor pair; the README describes where task families, actor profiles and a later sensor profile plug in.

## Limits

Single training seed per fork; two evaluation seeds, with the effect not surviving the second; one ancestry group; known map with ideal localization; no held-out layouts. Ten launches failed at startup with CUDA out-of-memory while another user's 20 GB job was resident and were rerun; one long task was killed by hand at 38 min under contention and rerun. Completion counts exclude no attempts.

## Next

1. Short-cycle navigation DAgger: switch at 10–30% of the reference clock on every task each cycle, refit, repeat, with the panel evaluated after each cycle.
2. Braking-aware supervision: weight approach/hold rows or add a terminal-speed auxiliary target; the overshoot mode is the one the data already covers.
3. A coherent re-entry continuation (walk-turn-stop bank) before more late-switch collection; late switches yield almost nothing under the nominal clock.
4. Fresh motion families and independently built layouts before any generalization claim.
