# Six-context closed-loop development comparison

The six-context learner and the script selected on the same expanded development data each pass all six contexts. Their five-context predecessors and the selected constant prior each pass five; walking passes two. Adding the complementary teaching encounter therefore produces a usable learned response, with a measured tradeoff: the new learner and script each take 0.50 seconds longer on the short and long passages than their respective predecessors.

This is a completed **development data-extension and script-retuning comparison**, comprising 36 distinct actual policy–context executions at physics seed 8732. All six layouts were inspected during development. There is one evaluation seed, no independently acquired curriculum comparison, and no held-out traversal or navigation claim.

The six-context learner retains the same 114D inputs, three phase-specific value heads and selected ridge penalty of 10; its teaching set adds the complementary seven branches. The six-context script repeats the same ordered 72-setting search on all six development contexts. The original models, selection artifacts and 20 actual episodes remain unchanged. The new 16 episodes comprise both updated policies on all six contexts, plus the original four policies on the complement. They complete all 36 policy–context pairs without rerunning the earlier 20.

Both updated policies wait at reference phases 0.30 and 1.00 seconds, then request sustained adaptation at 1.40 seconds in the complement, short and long passages. They use the prior derivative at 1.00 seconds in the early beam and two-beam contexts. In empty space the learner still commits to the late prior derivative, while the script remains neutral. Their measured passage times differ by one 0.02-second tick there and match on every obstacle. This does not establish a meaningful learned advantage over the strong script.

The extra successful complement is an actual result at seed 8732: 4.54 seconds. The 4.20-second sustained teacher execution at seed 8731 is separate evidence. It is not a lookup replacement for the policy outcome. Relative to each five-context predecessor, the updated policy adds 0.50 seconds on each of the short and long passages, for a mean increase of 0.20 seconds over the five mutually successful contexts. The added data repairs a passage deficit while leaving avoidable cost errors. Replay weighting, scene construction and architecture effects are not isolated by this comparison.
