\section{Teacher Initialization and Decision-Learning Details}
\label{app:initialization}
If a phase has no consequential row, initialization requires a complete matched
table with at least two legal actions, all required continuations admitted, and
all legal actions passing with exactly equal finite nonnegative passage times.
Zero ridge coefficients and intercepts then solve the measured zero-regret
targets. Normalization uses those rows with standard-deviation floor 0.05; only
supported legal columns are trained. The deterministic lowest-index argmax can
choose WAIT. Once consequential data exist, they alone determine the fit.
Missing or all-failed tables do not initialize a head. This common rule was added
after the first empty bootstrap exposed an exact cost tie at 1.00 s; the
previously selected ridge penalty 10 was retained. Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}
records the corresponding acquisition versions.

The September 9 decision-learning comparison uses all 42 existing development
branches and leaves all phases of one context together in each fold. It compares
the unchanged ridge with separate depth-two feasibility and time trees, both
with deterministic seed zero. Feasibility uses measured failures as well as
passes; time uses passes only. The loss and model family change jointly, so this
comparison does not isolate nonlinearity. Exact feature keys canonicalize signed
zero and otherwise preserve every stored float64 value. No measurement tolerance
is imposed. All six keys differ at all three phases. Information partitions must
refine over phases; full-state prefix hashes and scene identifiers are excluded
from the observation-equivalence rule.

The replay comparison fits the same ridge on the same training portion of each
fold. Each arm starts from a uniform fit and receives three refits. Current-error
priority is recomputed exclusively on that fold's training rows. Coverage uses
the joint-bin definition in the main text. Held-out decisions never enter
normalization, priorities or fitting. All four arms have four passing branch
proxies out of six. Their mean consequential phase regrets are 0.18952 for
uniform, coverage-only and uniform/coverage, and 0.19570 for current error.
These controls balance encounters before phases, whereas the original ridge
comparison weights available contexts equally within each phase; the two
uniform conventions therefore need not have identical phase regret.

Neither CPU experiment adds physical execution. The existing teacher and model
artifacts remain unchanged. Full per-fold choices, costs and replay weights are
stored with the scripts \texttt{motion2scene\_decision\_study.py} and
\texttt{motion2scene\_replay\_comparison.py}; the project development-learning
report links the exact result files.

The completed M2 acquisition controls extend this comparison to three acquired
corpora. Uniform, coverage-only, the actual 0.8-uniform/0.2-coverage fallback,
historical gated gaps, historical ungated gaps, refreshed supervised error and
historical-plus-error weights each select passing recorded branches on five of
the same six development contexts in every corpus. Every corpus retains three
positive historical phase gaps from its first acquisition student. All six
nonempty phase cue records per corpus satisfy the current visibility rule, and
gated and ungated replay weights are identical. Thus replay priority is active,
but this checkpoint does not exercise a visibility exclusion or establish a
passage gain. The historical gaps remain attached to their pre-update generating
models. The refreshed residuals measure supervised fitting error. The 21 final
control models require 66 complete policy fits: one common initial fit and
three refits for each of seven controls, per corpus. These are CPU fitting
operations, with no new physical evaluation.

The same seven controls are now complete at M4 for all three original corpora.
All again select passing recorded branches in 5/6 contexts. Within each corpus,
every control selects the same complete prior schedule on all six contexts:
entry at 1.00 s for seeds 93201 and 93203, and 0.30 s for seed 93202. Relative to
uniform, selected-schedule changes and passing-branch differences are zero;
recorded time differences are also zero on the five mutually successful contexts
per comparison. Each corpus retains three positive historical phase gaps, and
all twelve nonempty phase cues satisfy the current visibility rule. Gated and
ungated weights remain identical despite differences between replay and uniform
weights. Re-evaluation of the 42 saved M2/M4 controls reproduces all 252 recorded
context choices and 126 weight recipes without new fitting or physics. The
checkpoints share nested training prefixes and the same six development
contexts, so these are repeated readouts rather than independent policy trials.
This extends the recorded-input replay null result; its separate closed-loop
benefit remains unestablished.

\subsection{Secondary learner comparison}
\label{app:learner_debug}
The regression already appears on recorded teaching inputs: the six-context
ridge selects sustained schedules on the short and long passages, with branch
times 4.06 and 4.64 s. We compare it with depth-two decision trees predicting
feasibility and successful passage time separately. Each legal action has a
feasibility classifier; its time regressor uses successful observations only.
Among actions with estimated passage probability at least 0.5, choose the
fastest; if none qualifies, choose maximum estimated feasibility. Depth and
threshold are fixed before this comparison, with no parameter search.

Both models select passing recorded branches in all six training contexts. The
tree reduces mean regret over the 17 consequential phase tables from 0.01916 to
0.00713 and recovers the 4.48 s prior schedule on the long passage, but selects a
slower 4.20 s schedule on the two-beam context. Leaving each entire context out
gives both learners four passing branch proxies out of six; both miss the early
and complementary contexts when those teaching examples are withheld.
Thus the alternative improves some fitted decisions without establishing a
generalization advantage. These are finite recorded-branch readouts, with no new
closed-loop executions in the fitting comparison.

We then execute the same fixed tree on all six contexts at the policy-comparison
seed 8732. Its exported decisions reproduce the original tree exactly, and its
first neutral inputs match the ridge recordings in every context. The tree passes
four contexts, losing the long and complementary passages to measured contact;
ridge passes all six. Tree passage times are 5.90, 4.06, 2.72 and 4.12 s on the
empty, short, early and two-beam contexts, respectively. Over these four mutually
successful contexts it is 0.12 s slower on average. All six measurements are
complete. On the complementary context, a small change in the distant corridor's
unknown fraction crosses a feasibility-tree threshold and causes immediate short
commitment instead of WAIT. Thus lower fitted regret does not resolve the
closed-loop cost/capability tradeoff. We retain ridge as the common acquisition
learner and expand the teaching corpus rather than selecting this alternative.

\section{Sensor Sensitivity Implementation and Development Check}
\label{app:sensors}
At each capture, dropout removes fan channels independently without adding
free-space evidence for missing measurements. Gaussian noise perturbs detected
surface ranges; a distance outside the sensor interval becomes a missing return.
Surviving normals and robot pose remain ideal. Fixed latency delays the corrupted
packet before its world-space rays update the observation history. Capture and
delivery times remain separate; the student receives neither clean rays nor
future packets. Random draws are indexed by corruption seed, frame and fixed
fan-channel position, independently of which channels hit a surface.

On recorded neutral prefixes from all six teaching contexts, zero perturbation
reconstructs all 18 decision vectors exactly. At corruption seed 95001, each of
10\%/30\% dropout, 1/3 cm range noise and 40/100 ms delay changes the short-context
branch proxy from sustained to prior, while preserving six passing proxies.
The separate native four-condition check uses the existing short scene, the
six-context policy, physics seed 8732 and corruption seed 95001. It records four
complete episodes, 4,768 physics steps, and 1,192 exactly reconstructed student
feature vectors. All conditions pass with zero measured beam force. Nominal,
30\% dropout and 3 cm noise retain sustained and take 4.10 s; 100 ms latency
selects prior and takes 3.60 s. Thus the dropout/noise proxy change from the
seed-8731 teaching capture does not reproduce in this seed-8732 execution.
This controlled development check exercises the perception interventions;
multi-layout, multi-seed sensitivity of the selected final policy remains part
of the broader evaluation.

\section{Historical Development and Design Decisions}
\label{app:history}
The original diagnostic manuscript, \texttt{paper.tex}, and every earlier
working draft remain unchanged or archived. Their results explain the present
interventions but do not supply an untouched evaluation of them.

\paragraph{The original command ceiling.}
The earlier 36-condition walk/d040 bank contains 14 both-pass, ten crouch-only,
12 both-fail and no walk-only conditions. Its maximum passage under those two
fixed commands is $24/36=66.7\%$, already attained by always-d040 and the analytic
selector. A learned selector misses two otherwise solvable conditions.
This separates missed decisions from missing capability: replacing a selector
cannot solve a condition for which every unchanged command fails. The bank was
extensively inspected during development and remains a regression benchmark.

\paragraph{Execution and observation interventions.}
Four-second development experiments qualified additional authored crouch
references and legal entry times. A newly constructed beam defeated walk and d040
while d055, d070 and d085 passed. The old low beam still defeated every tested
option. These references are \texttt{local\_crouch} edits of a generated neutral
source, not independently learned skills. Their old fixed return windows do not
qualify the present six-second durations.

Identical earlier 214-dimensional inputs sometimes have conflicting walking
labels. A larger deterministic classifier cannot distinguish identical inputs.
All members of the observed ambiguous classes remained passable with the
conservative adaptation; the ambiguity concerned unnecessary adaptation rather
than unavoidable passage failure. The later floor/ceiling history preserves
information that a single surface-height value discards, but does not guarantee
unique task labels, semantic obstacle identity or timely underside observations.

A six-second 106D duration experiment executes three options on three scenes:
empty permits all, a short beam permits short/sustained, and a long passage permits
sustained only. Its separate 15-policy evaluation at seed 8732 has learned and
scripted policies both passing three of three with identical times 5.40/4.10/4.68 s;
both select sustained on the short beam although constant short passes in 4.06 s.
Constant sustained fails the empty finite-horizon hold without contact or fall.
These outcomes establish a duration distinction and an integration limitation;
changes from 106D/0.5 s memory to 114D/2 s memory are not a controlled sensor
ablation. Earlier four-second phase-head data updates yield a one-control-tick
cost change on one development condition, while a shared-head update regresses
aggregate cost. Neither is a primary curriculum result.

\paragraph{Learned proposals remain a negative development result.}
An earlier learned conditional proposal uses paired executed summaries and a
15-frame approach history. In its final geometric pilot it accepts 41 of 192
proposals against 123 of 192 for analytic resampling and zero for uniform sampling.
This pilot neither demonstrates neural acquisition advantage nor measures the
new curriculum's held-out traversal. The present primary method uses an analytic
constructor. A future conditional proposal could approximate its useful regions;
a future adversary could edit geometry while retaining a physically verified
solution. Neither extension is executed in the primary fixed candidate queues.

\paragraph{Prior sample and authored derivative.}
The installed Kimodo constrained experiment generates one six-second sample
using the existing checkpoint. It violates native joint limits and proposed join
checks; the raw sample is retained as rejected. An explicitly registered operator
projects 11 violating joint samples and splices the qualified neutral prefix and
tail with smooth transitions, without reinference or hidden clipping. Native
kinematic flags, including foot-ground geometry and loader interpolation at the
splice boundary, remain recorded. The derived reference is admitted only after
complete physical schedule qualification. Its successful early-beam response
cannot be attributed to unmodified neural generation alone. A separate CPU SONIC
planner sample establishes only installed inference and conversion; it is not a
physically qualified member of the current option bank.

\paragraph{Contact and scorer revisions.}
The original strict net-contact predicate rejected the six-second motions because
of inherited hip--wrist contacts, including in neutral walking. A separately
registered counterpart experiment measures self-contact and environmental contact
independently; new physical captures qualify under the stated external-contact
criterion. Old strict failures remain failures under their original predicate.
No criterion revision is presented as an unchanged safety contract.

The single-beam scorer initially omitted the declared initial-upstream condition
already checked by courses. The explicit repair requires every initial body origin
to lie upstream of the beam entry plane. With complete measurement/source
admission, its violation is named \texttt{invalid\_initial\_approach}, a task
failure distinct from collision or fall; missing evidence remains unknown.
The applied repair passes 70 focused tests. Independent replay of 45 prior
recordings changes no passage, time or classifier outcome; their minimum initial
upstream margin is 0.842 m. Subsequent common learner/runtime freezes bind the
new scorer, while original execution snapshots remain preserved.
