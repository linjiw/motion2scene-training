# Decision boundary

This is an exploratory engineering study. Its declared primary metric was finite-grid geometric critical-location probability, not physical passage. No new population inference or non-inferiority claim is made.

| Question | Decision | Evidence and next permitted step |
|---|---|---|
| Can a categorical critical-location model concentrate probability on this bank’s withheld coordinates? | SUPPORTED FOR THE REGISTERED ESTIMAND | 46.18% mean critical mass versus 1.60% uniform, restricted to this declared finite geometric domain. No physical interpretation. |
| Does motion conditioning add substantial generalizable value? | INCONCLUSIVE | Unconditional reaches 44.44%; one shared bank does not establish source transfer. Acquire/verify independent source ancestry before a new transfer test. |
| Does contrastive ranking reduce geometric penetration-witness mass? | SUPPORTED FOR THE REGISTERED ESTIMAND | 33.79% to 22.32% on the declared coordinate test; exact descriptive probabilities. Critical mass is slightly lower, so non-degradation is not established. |
| Does masked completion improve critical-location probability? | INCONCLUSIVE | Its point estimate is slightly lower at both seeds. This configuration does not support the proposed improvement; two initialization seeds do not establish a population effect. |
| Does combining both losses provide the best model? | INCONCLUSIVE | Lower mean critical mass and substantial seed variation. No combined-model promotion. |
| Does the new model improve contact-qualified passage beyond Screened Uniform? | BLOCKED/UNTESTED | No new physical labels or matched downstream evaluation. Geometric screened-uniform here is an oracle query baseline, not the missing pilot comparator. |
| May Phase 3 or hardware claims advance? | BLOCKED/UNTESTED | This study does not resolve the prior interrupted pilot or waive any adoption gate. |
