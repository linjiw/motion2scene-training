# Construction from complete timed executions

The six-second development constructor uses the measured neutral, short and sustained executions, including entry and recovery. It proposes overhead passages where one available schedule keeps a finite clearance margin and another has nominal geometric interference. The [screen result](/home/linjiw/research-data/groot-wbc/m2s-timed-scene-screen-v1/result.json) is proposal evidence, not a physical label or an observation guarantee.

The frozen [registration](/home/linjiw/research-data/groot-wbc/m2s-timed-scene-screen-v1/registration.json) precedes its 1,305 grid queries: 15 normalized route stations from 0.40 to 0.75, lengths 0.10/0.50/1.00 m, and 29 underside heights from 1.20 to 1.34 m. Width is 1.20 m and thickness 0.10 m. Beam placement follows the named six-second neutral route and its net heading. These are development layouts; the reserved V2/V3 geometries are not inputs.

The positive envelope contains the native robot geometry; the negative witness uses only native primitive subsets. Both are transformed by 298 recorded body poses at 50 Hz. No target-reference envelope substitutes for execution. The finite uncertainty set has 81 world-coordinate combinations: XY offsets each ±0.02 m, underside ±0.005 m, and yaw ±0.02 rad, with zero included in every dimension. Positive clearance must be at least 0.01 m over every offset; the negative witness must have clearance at most −0.01 m at nominal placement. This deliberately differs from requiring negative interference at every offset.

There are 548 nominal pair contrasts, of which 443 retain the specified positive margin. The script chooses maximum minimum slack separately for each positive/negative pair and beam length, with deterministic first-index ties. Nine scenes are selected. Short-versus-sustained contrasts exist in the direction where sustained clears and short interferes; no selected scene establishes a short-only capability.

The run records 52,218 whole-trajectory primitive-clearance queries and 24.57 seconds of measured construction time. An AABB distance lower bound safely prunes capsules that cannot reduce a 0.20 m capped clearance. The cap does not affect either 0.01 m acceptance boundary. Randomized comparison against the full primitive query found no discrepancy; these checks establish the implementation of the primitive calculation, not continuous-time collision safety.

Observation-aware admission uses actual nearest ray endpoints and causal delivery timestamps. The [timing module](../../gear_sonic/dataset_generation/hallucination/motion2scene_observation_timing.py) associates measured endpoints with the natively audited beam surface and separately identifies underside returns with measured downward normals. Geometry is a privileged audit input and never becomes a student feature. Generic upper occupancy from another wall does not count as observing the beam. The audit preserves late-visible outcomes for challenge analysis and conservative-response training.

Visibility alone does not prove universal cost distinguishability or noisy-sensor performance.
